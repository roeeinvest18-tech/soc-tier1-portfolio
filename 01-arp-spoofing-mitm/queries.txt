Wireshark display filters used, in the order applied during the investigation:

1. arp.opcode == 2
   -> Isolate ARP replies; surfaced "IP duplicate" warnings.

2. eth.dst == aa:bb:cc:00:00:20
   -> Map every destination that received ARP traffic from the suspect MAC
      (victim scope).

3. arp.opcode == 2 and arp.src.proto_ipv4 == 192.168.1.1
   -> Confirm two different MAC addresses claiming to own the gateway IP.

4. arp.opcode == 1 and arp.dst.proto_ipv4 == 192.168.1.1
   -> Isolate genuine ARP requests for the gateway, to cross-reference
      against replies and separate legitimate unicast replies from
      unsolicited ones.

Note: aa:bb:cc:00:00:20 and 192.168.1.1 are the values as recorded in the
original lab notes (a private lab subnet / placeholder MAC range, not a real
production network).
