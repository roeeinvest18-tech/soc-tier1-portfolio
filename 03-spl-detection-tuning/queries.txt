SPL rules as written, one per tuning scenario:

1. Egress volume - SRV-BKP-01
index=alon sourcetype=proxy
| eval hour=tonumber(strftime(_time,"%H"))
| where NOT (hour>=1 AND hour<3)
| stats sum(bytes_out) as total by src_host
| where total > 10000000000

2. Lockout storm - svc_sql
(Logic as written, not a runnable SPL string in the source:)
Look up recent help-desk tickets for the same user before alerting on the
account lockout storm.

3. Mass execution - ccmexec
index=alon sourcetype=winevent EventCode=4688 cmdline!="ccmexec.exe /deploy KB5041585"
| bin _time span=10m

Note: "index=alon" refers to the training dataset's index name, not a real
company/environment identifier.
